| **Maker Activity** | Finished PCB layouts & simulations run | 5,000 / mo | 25,000 / mo |
| **Ideation Velocity** | Stage Zero workbooks completed | 1,000 / mo | 5,000 / mo |
| **Problem Tackling** | Unsolved problems claimed by active squads | 50 squads | 250 squads |
