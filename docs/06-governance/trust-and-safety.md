# Trust and Safety Framework

## Zero Tolerance Policies

1. **Child Endangerment & Exploitation:**
   - Any attempt to bypass minor-adult communication restrictions, solicit personal contact details, or harass minors results in immediate account termination, IP ban, and reporting to legal authorities under the DPDP Act and POCSO Act.
2. **Weapons & Harmful Fabrication:**
   - Schematics, 3D models, or firmware intended for firearms, lethal weaponry, chemical/biological synthesis, or malicious network exploitation are strictly prohibited.
3. **Astroturfing & Plagiarism:**
   - Copying another creator's hardware layout or research paper without attribution triggers automatic badge revocation and public audit marks on the offender's Innovation Passport.
