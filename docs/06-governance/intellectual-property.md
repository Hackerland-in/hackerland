# Intellectual Property & Open Licensing

Hackerland champions open innovation while giving creators total control over their intellectual property:

1. **Default Open Hardware & Software Licensing:**
   - Creators are encouraged to release schematics and firmware under recognized open-source licenses:
     - **Hardware:** CERN Open Hardware Licence (CERN-OHL-S/W/P v2) or TAPR Open Hardware License.
     - **Software:** MIT, Apache 2.0, or AGPLv3.
     - **Documentation:** Creative Commons CC-BY-SA 4.0.
2. **Private Stage Zero Ideation:**
   - Ideas in the IdeaLab remain 100% private and encrypted to the creator until they choose to publish them to the community.
3. **Patent Defense & Prior Art Defense:**
   - Publicly logged inventions on Hackerland establish timestamped prior art, protecting indie inventors from predatory patent trolls.
