# Community Governance & Meritocracy

Hackerland operates as a community-driven meritocracy governed by transparent rules:

1. **Karma & Proof of Work (Not Hype):**
   - Community standing is earned exclusively through tangible actions: shipping verified hardware prototypes, passing Design Rule Checks (DRC), publishing reproducible build logs, and mentoring junior builders.
2. **Squad Autonomy:**
   - Each project squad determines its own internal roadmap, contribution licenses (e.g. CERN-OHL, MIT, Apache 2.0), and squad membership.
3. **Escalation & Dispute Resolution:**
   - Multi-tiered moderation system combining automated safety filters, elected community peer reviewers, and staff mediation for bounty escrow disputes.
