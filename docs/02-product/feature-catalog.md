# Feature Catalog: The 10 High-Priority Ecosystem Engines

### 1. Unsolved Problems Library (`/problems`)
- Rigorously vetted repository of grand engineering, healthcare, energy, and social problems.
- Spec sheets defining key physical constraints, target impact metrics, and active squad claims.

### 2. Build Journey Archive & Decision Archaeology (`/stories`)
- Interactive non-linear retrospective case studies (e.g. SpaceX Falcon 9, Ather Energy 450, NVIDIA).
- "What Would You Do?" interactive decision forks with real historical tradeoff analyses.

### 3. Stage Zero IdeaLab (`/ideas`)
- 7-stage guided ideation workbook: Notice, Choose, Understand, Define, Prototype, Test, Plan.
- Instant AI review assessing physical feasibility, market demand, and existing open-source prior art.

### 4. In-Browser Hardware PCB Studio (`/manufacturing/pcb`)
- Complete A-to-Z circuit design suite running client-side with zero install.
- Real-time DRC engine, spice-like netlist tracer, live 3D photorealistic renderer, and RS-274X Gerber export.

### 5. Multibody Physics & Space Simulation Suite (`/manufacturing/physics`, `/manufacturing/iss`)
- In-browser Newtonian and orbital dynamics engines.
- Real-time telemetry visualization for rockets, electric vehicles, drones, and satellites.

### 6. Personal Innovation Passport (`/passport`)
- Cryptographically verifiable proof-of-work records.
- Verifiable credentials for hardware prototypes fabricated, peer-reviewed pull requests, and safety certifications.

### 7. Reverse Engineering & Teardown Library
- High-resolution component breakdowns, bill-of-materials (BOM) cost teardowns, and schematic reconstructions.

### 8. Squad Matchmaking & Collaboration Engine
- Skill-weighted squad matching algorithm pairing hardware designers, firmware coders, and field researchers.
- Embedded milestone escrow contracts and bounty disbursements.

### 9. Manufacturing Vendor Network
- Instant fabrication RFQ routing to verified Indian and global fabrication plants.
- Automated DFM (Design for Manufacturability) automated rule verification.

### 10. Young Innovators Protected Track
- COPPA / India DPDP Act compliant sandboxed workspaces.
- Age-bracketed safety fences preventing unsolicited direct messaging while fostering maker curiosity.
