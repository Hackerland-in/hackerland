# Hackerland: Product Principles

1. **Creation Over Consumption:** Reward completed work, prototypes, build logs, and active learning rather than endless scrolling or vanity engagement.
2. **Knowledge Over Virality:** Useful, structured knowledge (decision archaeology, failure autopsies, component breakdowns) remains discoverable and actionable for years.
3. **Open by Default:** Build logs, schematics, and roadmaps are open, so every project accelerates the next builder.
4. **Evidence Over Claims:** Distinguish peer-verified contributions, merged work, and tested hardware from self-reported resume bullet points.
5. **Low Floor, High Ceiling:** Accessible to a 10-year-old curious beginner with zero technical background, with depth that supports PhD researchers and hardware veterans.
6. **Safety & Trust by Design:** Age-appropriate experiences, strict protection for minors under DPDP frameworks, verified mentor checks, and transparent escrow.
